3. **Pi voltage criterion.** `WORKPLAN.md` and `WIRING.md` §9 ask for **≥ 5.1 V at the Pi pins**. Our RMRC UBEC is selectable 5 / 6 / 7.4 V only, and **5.0 V is the right setting**. 6 V would exceed the Pi's 5 V input, so never use it. A 5.0 V regulator can't reliably show ≥ 5.1 V. **Proposal:** G4 and G5 pass on `vcgencmd get_throttled` = `0x0` throughout, plus ≥ 4.9 V at pins 2/14 with the camera running. If they fail: shorter or thicker UBEC→Pi leads, a better joint, or a smaller load. Do not raise the UBEC setting.
