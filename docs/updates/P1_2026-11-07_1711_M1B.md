## Then — G1 cold checks and G2 first power
- **G1 (meter, no battery):**
  - The resistance across the XT60 climbs as the capacitor charges and does not sit at ≈0 Ω.
  - Continuity on every joint.
  - The pack's XT60 polarity matches the aircraft lead.
  - No short across the UBEC output.
  - UBEC output is **open** (not connected) to FC 5V / 4V5 / Vx.
- **G2 (first power):** connect only the FC stack, the ESC (prop off, signal plug unplugged) and the UBEC with no load. Record:
  - pack voltage
  - FC 5V pad (expect 5.2 ± 0.1 V)
  - 4V5 (expect 4.5–5.0 V)
  - Vx servo rail (expect 5.0 ± 0.1 V)
  - UBEC output (expect ≈5.0 V)
  - whether anything is warm at 60 s
